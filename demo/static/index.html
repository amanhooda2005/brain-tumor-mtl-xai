Explanation methods on the same models

From slice to answer

  1. Standardise the sliceGrayscale, resized to 384 × 384 and normalised the way the pretrained encoder expects.
  2. Shared encoderA ResNet-34, first trained on ImageNet, turns the slice into features at five scales.
  3. Draw the tumourA U-Net decoder with attention rebuilds a full-size tumour mask from those features.
  4. Name the tumourThe classifier pools features over the slice and under the predicted mask, so segmentation tells it where to look.
  5. Explain and checkGrad-CAM and HiResCAM show which regions drove the decision; with an expert mask, the overlap is measured instead of eyeballed.
